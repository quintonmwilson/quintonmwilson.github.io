Visitor Forecasting for Surf City, NC

Client project · UNC capstone

Surf City faces unpredictable tourism surges that strain staffing and public resources. As the machine learning developer on a four-person team working with Blue Nalu Analytics, I helped replace the city's SARIMA model with daily visitor forecasts that emergency managers can use to staff ahead of surges.

  • XGBoost model explained 94% of variance in daily out-of-market visitors (R² = 0.936), with 10.5% MAPE and RMSE of about 3,300 visitors
  • Benchmarked XGBoost and Random Forest against a transparent weighted year-over-year growth baseline, so stakeholders could see what each model added
  • Engineered 25+ features from five years of daily foot traffic, event, weather and market data: lags chosen by ACF, PACF and AIC analysis, 7-day rolling averages, cyclical date encodings and holiday flags
  • Found that recent visitor history and seasonality drive predictions, while weather contributes little, which makes the forecasts stable enough for operational planning

Financial Fraud Detection

Team project

With two classmates, compared XGBoost, Random Forest and a neural network for detecting fraud in highly imbalanced transaction data, aiming to raise recall without driving up false positives.

  • Tested SMOTE oversampling, undersampling, cost-sensitive learning and decision-threshold tuning against baseline models
  • XGBoost delivered the best fraud-class F1 (0.87) and ROC-AUC of 0.998
  • XGBoost trained roughly 100x faster than the neural network (4s vs. 415s)
